Which chunks come back, and why

Runs in the browser. BM25 and a hashed embedding over a fictional clinic handbook. No model call and no API key.

Northline Dental Group wants a help-desk bot over its HR and IT articles. Before anyone blames the model, this page shows which chunks a question actually retrieves, and which words pulled them up.

Question and index

0 is BM25 only. 1 is the hashed embedding only. Scores are scaled per query before they are mixed, the same idea as a hybrid alpha.

    k1 = 1.2 and b = 0.75 are the Lucene defaults. Lower the dimensions and unrelated words start sharing buckets.

    All demos · The handbook is fictional. The scorers are the ones described in the folder README.
