Something new most mornings

Small experiments with AI

Each morning I pick one idea from the AI world, a new paper or a habit dev teams are picking up, and build a little page you can play with. Nothing to install. Just open one and click around.

  1. 4 Nov 2026

    Rules after compaction

    Northwind Logistics lets an ops agent send email and open tickets, and the handbook allows only @northwind.example. After a scripted compact, the agent CCs an outside vendor unless the rule is pinned again or the tool wrapper blocks the domain.

    Open demo →
  2. 3 Nov 2026

    WebMCP storefront

    Alpine Outfitters registers catalog and cart tools on the open page, and a scripted shopping agent calls them. Next to it, a DOM scraper reads CSS classes, takes more steps, and returns the wrong shoe after a layout change.

    Open demo →
  3. 2 Nov 2026

    Nestor: nested sparsity

    A one-block toy of Nestor (Chu, Pai, and Ma, arXiv:2610.31818). The page uses random routers so you can see the wiring. It is not the trained model.

    Open demo →
  4. 1 Nov 2026

    Structured Outputs Playground

    A simulated token-by-token decoder for a support ticket and a vendor invoice. A schema mask blocks illegal JSON as it is written, next to an unconstrained draft that breaks, with a validator on both.

    Open demo →
  5. 31 Oct 2026

    RAG Retrieval Visualizer

    A browser page over a fictional dental-group handbook. It chunks the articles, scores them with BM25 and a hashed embedding, and shows which passage a help-desk question actually retrieves.

    Open demo →
