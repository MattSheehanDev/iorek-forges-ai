What ARCBench measured

These rates are from the paper. This page does not measure them. violation@3 counts a case if any of three runs breaks the rule. The 2.6× figure is a different cut: the share of runs.

9.6% → 37.8%

violation@3, full history to after compaction

Study S1, all four models. 8.9 points also fail with the full history. 28.9 appear only after compaction. Compacted runs still retain the rule 70.1% of the time, keep it in part 26.9%, and lose it 3.0%.

2.6×

retained-rule runs versus full history

21.1% of compacted runs that still contain the rule violate, against 8.1% of full-history runs. Keeping the sentence and obeying it are different events. Partial retention violates on 29.4% of those runs. Absence violates on 83.3%.

91.2% kept

generative summaries retain the rule

Generative strategies reach 21.8% violation@3, with the rule partial in 7.8% of runs and absent in 0.9%. Selective compactors lose the rule in 84.1% of runs and reach 50.2% violation@3. FIFO loses it in every run.

        The badges are a script, written so the failure is easy to see. It refuses on the full history and when the rule is pinned after the summary. It sends the vendor CC when the only rule text it has is the summary, including when that summary quotes the handbook. ARCBench’s models do not snap back that cleanly. Under the escape attack, rule preservation put the rule back in 99.0% of runs and brought violation@6 from 58.8% down to 29.4%, still above the 23.5% no-attack runs in that study. The tool gate is ordinary code. It reads the addresses on the call.

        What a team would change

        Pin it outside the summary

        Put the hard rule in a slot the compactor is not allowed to rewrite: a system block, a handbook file, or a policy tool the agent has to read again. Re-inject those same words after every compact, including a manual /compact. In the paper, that restore is strong on the text and partial on the behavior.

        Check it where the tool runs

        The wrapper on send_email looks at to, cc, and bcc. Any domain other than northwind.example returns an error, and nothing is sent. The attempt still counts.

        function isInternal(email) {

        Grade the call

        A dashboard that asks whether the summary still contains the sentence will miss the retained-rule failures. Log the arguments the wrapper saw. Some teams also skip the paraphrase and search the original turns when a later step needs the rule. That is a different design. ARCBench measures compaction.
